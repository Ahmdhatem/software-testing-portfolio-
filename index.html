Testing Process

How I Approach Software Testing

01

Understand Requirements

Clarify expected behavior, acceptance criteria, and business rules.

02

Identify Risks

Prioritize critical workflows, edge cases, and failure-prone areas.

03

Design Tests

Create clear scenarios, positive/negative cases, and traceable test cases.

04

Execute

Test systematically and compare actual behavior with expected results.

05

Report Defects

Document reproducible bugs with evidence, severity, priority, and clear steps.

06

Retest & Regress

Verify fixes and confirm that existing functionality remains stable.

07

Final Validation

Confirm the product satisfies requirements and is ready for release.

My goal is not only to find bugs, but to understand why they happen and help prevent them from reaching users.
